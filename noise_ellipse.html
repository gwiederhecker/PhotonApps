Model: the tip displacement is z(t) = A+eiΩt + A−e−iΩt with slowly varying random amplitudes satisfying ⟨|A±|²⟩ = n± and ⟨A+A−⟩ = M. Then Sp = n++n−+2 Re M, Sq = n++n−−2 Re M, Cpq = 2 Im M, D = n+−n− (shot noise = 1). The state is physical when its smaller symplectic eigenvalue ν− = √(SpSq − Cpq²) − |D| ≥ 1, which is stronger than det Γ ≥ 1; the purity is 1/det Γ with det Γ = SpSq − Cpq² − D². A homodyne detector assumes D = 0 and so reports a lower purity.
