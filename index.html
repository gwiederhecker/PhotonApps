PhotonApps

A growing set of small browser applets for photonics and quantum optics — resonators, noise, detection, and whatever else is easier to understand by moving a slider than by reading an equation. Each one is a single HTML file: open it and play.

These applets were built with substantial help from large language models. The underlying physics is textbook material, but the code was drafted by an LLM and then read, corrected and spot-checked against analytic limits by hand. It has not been formally verified.

So treat them as instruments for building intuition and for teaching, not as reference implementations. If a curve looks wrong, it may well be wrong: please open an issue.

The applets

Nothing to install and no server: the whole app is the page you land on, and it runs entirely in your browser.

Resonators

Linear cavity physics: transmission, dispersion and coupled rings.

  • Ring resonator: the cavity response

    Transmission and intracavity response of an all-pass ring coupled to a bus waveguide, as a function of detuning, coupling and intrinsic loss.

    Ring resonator_ the cavity response.html
  • Resonator dispersion: D1 and D2

    Expansion of the resonance frequencies around a pump mode: free spectral range D1, group-velocity dispersion D2, and the integrated dispersion they produce.

    Resonator dispersion_ D₁ and D₂.html
  • SiN coupled microrings

    Two interactive slides on a pair of coupled silicon nitride microrings: hybridised supermodes and the splitting set by the inter-ring coupling.

    SiN coupled microrings – two interactive slides (16_10).html
  • Resonator detection

    A detuned cavity used as a quadrature analyser. The cavity rotates the noise ellipse of the incoming field by a detuning-dependent angle, turning phase fluctuations into intensity fluctuations that a direct detector can read. Shows how the rotation angle and the measured variance depend on detuning, linewidth and loss.

    resonator_detection.html

Nonlinear optics

Phase matching, parametric gain and Kerr frequency combs.

  • Quasi-phase matching

    How periodic poling compensates the phase mismatch of a χ(2) process, and how the generated field grows with and without it.

    Quasi-phase matching — interactive slide.html
  • Parametric gain: dispersion vs Kerr shift

    Four-wave-mixing gain set by the balance between linear phase mismatch from dispersion and the nonlinear phase shift from the pump.

    Parametric gain_ dispersion vs Kerr shift.html
  • Kerr microcombs: from CW to dissipative solitons

    The evolution of a pumped Kerr microresonator as the laser is tuned across resonance, from the continuous-wave state through modulation instability to dissipative Kerr solitons.

    Kerr microcombs_ from CW to dissipative solitons.html

Quantum noise

Squeezed states and how detection sees them.

  • Parametric pendulum: squeezing

    A mechanical analogue of squeezing: a pendulum whose length is modulated at twice its natural frequency amplifies one quadrature and de-amplifies the other.

    Parametric pendulum_ squeezing.html
  • Noise ellipse

    The quadrature noise ellipse of a Gaussian state in the x–p plane. Set the squeezing and anti-squeezing levels, the orientation of the squeezed axis and the detection efficiency, and follow the variance a homodyne detector would record as the local-oscillator phase is swept — including how loss and phase jitter fill the ellipse back towards the vacuum circle.

    noise_ellipse.html

To run them locally, clone the repository and open any of the HTML files directly in a browser. There is no build step, and nothing is sent anywhere.
